# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vue 3, TypeScript and Vite, selected by the user. FastAPI serves the built interface and API from the same address. SQLite and filesystem storage run inside one Python process.

## Users

People converting text and long articles into downloadable speech, including creators adjusting voices and expression. The owner manages provider connections through a separate administrator page.

## Product Purpose

An anonymous, free-to-use TTS workspace with configurable Edge and Xiaomi MiMo channels, playback, downloads and recoverable background generation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

No membership, per-user daily quotas or domain binding. Channel capabilities come from the backend. MiMo offers preset, design and clone modes; Edge offers numeric prosody and subtitles. Resource limits protect the deployment. Upstream availability and pricing remain outside this service's control.

Tasks continue after the page closes, belong to the same anonymous browser, and expire seven days after completion by default. Only administrators can change channels or retention. Secrets remain on the server. Existing authenticated /v1 APIs remain supported.

## Brand Commitments

The user chose a light dubbing workspace: white editing surface, pale gray ground, dark teal primary actions, text and settings side by side on desktop and stacked on mobile. Reference sites are tts.vdj.me and ttsonline.cn; reuse the relevant workflows, not their quota or subscription system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable Chinese interface, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, associated labels, live status announcements and reduced-motion support.
