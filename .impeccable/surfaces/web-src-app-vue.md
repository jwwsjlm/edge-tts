# TTS workspace

Mode: Operate. Routes: / and /admin. The user confirmed a light, side-by-side workspace and direct access without sign-in.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Text, voice choice and finished audio form one visible workspace. No landing-page hero, pricing, decoration cards or subscription affordances.

OWN-WORLD: Cool pale gray shell, white editor, deep teal actions, subtle sage selection, charcoal Chinese system typography. Precise borders, generous editor space and compact parameter groups.

STORY: Paste text, pick an available channel and voice, adjust its real controls, submit, then listen or download. Anonymous history and expiration are explicit. Administration is separate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact brand/navigation header, page title and status, a wide left text editor with character count and actions, a narrower right voice panel. A result dock and ordered task list begin below. The signature interaction is real segment progress becoming a playable recording in the same dock, with a brief state-color transition.

FORM: User-pinned light workspace; desktop two-column 1fr/360px grid, mobile content first and controls second. seed: none (user-pinned). Standard native input semantics with consistent custom surfaces. Code-first, no imagery required.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
